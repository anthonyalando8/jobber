Needs review is always qualified: opportunity selection review ({{.Counts.OpportunityReview}}), composition semantic review ({{.Counts.CompositionReview}}), or reviewed drafts awaiting human approval ({{.Counts.ApprovalPending}}). Historical drafts are separate.

Opportunity counts are live postings: each one is in exactly one of strong, possible, unrelated or not yet assessed, so those four add up to the Opportunities total. {{.Counts.Suppressed}} suppressed postings (expired, filled or taken down) are excluded, as on the Opportunities page.
