Adding employer career boards

Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards are read from each employer's public job-board API.

Any board a discovered listing links to is picked up automatically. To follow an employer yourself, add it to ATS_BOARDS in the server environment, for example greenhouse:gitlab,lever:acme,ashby:example, and restart the app.
